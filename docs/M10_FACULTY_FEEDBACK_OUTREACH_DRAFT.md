Dear Professor [Last Name],

I wanted to share the results of an independent physical-design research investigation that grew out of my earlier FIFO RTL-to-GDSII work.

I studied whether the timing characteristics of surrounding address-generation logic can change which of two standard-cell RAM decoder architectures performs better after routing. Using ORRAM, OpenROAD, Yosys and extracted-parasitic static timing analysis, I observed context-dependent timing reversals and investigated their underlying critical paths. I also developed and prospectively tested an early architecture-selection model. The model showed some promising behavior on controlled examples, but its final synthesized-RTL evaluation did not outperform a simple baseline, which I have documented as an important limitation rather than presenting as a successful optimizer.

I have attached a two-page research brief and a detailed report with reproducible GitHub evidence. I would greatly appreciate your feedback on the validity of the results, the novelty of the question, and whether a stronger follow-up study would be worthwhile for doctoral research.
